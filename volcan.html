The Place

Volcán

Chiriquí Province · Tierras Altas · western Panama, near the Costa Rican border.

Volcán is a highland town in Panama’s coffee country—cooler than the coasts, agricultural in rhythm, and set along roads that connect Boquete, Cerro Punta, and the frontier.

The Tierras Altas are not exotic scenery for a brochure. They are a working landscape: farms, markets, schools, congregations already present, and the ordinary pressures of modern life arriving at their own pace. A church plant here must learn the place before it can speak to it.

Why here

We are going because a particular sending has a particular address. Volcán offers a setting where dwelling is possible: a climate that supports long residence, a region with room for a new congregation without pretending the gospel has never been heard, and a community small enough that presence cannot hide behind programming.

Departure for the field is planned for January 2027. What happens after arrival will be slower than fundraising copy usually admits—language, neighbors, worship, and the patient work of becoming known.

What we will not claim

We will not invent demographics to justify urgency, or paint the highlands as empty of Christian witness. Faithfulness does not require a vacuum. It requires a people willing to stay.
