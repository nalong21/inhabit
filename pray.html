Prayer

Ask with us

Prayer is not a substitute for sending; it is the first company we keep. Updates will appear here when there is something sober to report.

We ask for prayer as the family prepares to leave in January 2027, and afterward as a church takes shape in Volcán.

  • Preparation Clarity, patience, and integrity in the months of paperwork, partnership, and leave-taking.
  • Language and neighbors Humility to learn Spanish and local custom before assuming we have much to teach.
  • A people gathered That a congregation would form around Word and Table—not around personality or imported preferences.
  • Open doors without thin walls Hospitality that welcomes the stranger while keeping a thick, recognizable Christian pattern of life.
  • Endurance Grace to stay when the work is slow, and wisdom to repent when we are wrong.
